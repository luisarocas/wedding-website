Classic Madrid

A Few Favorite Stops

Retiro Park

Great for walking around, especially if the weather's nice. If you have time, don't miss the lake and the Crystal Palace.

Learn more

Prado Museum

If you'd like a classic Madrid museum visit, the Prado is one of the city's essentials and an easy choice for a few hours.

Visit site

Royal Palace

One of the city's most iconic landmarks, especially if you want to see grand architecture in the historic center.

Learn more

Temple of Debod

A lovely stop, especially later in the day. The setting is peaceful and it's one of the nicest places in Madrid for sunset views.

Learn more

Riu Plaza España Rooftop

Head up to the 360º Rooftop Bar for panoramic views across Madrid. It's especially good around sunset.

Visit site

Plaza Mayor

A historic square in the heart of old Madrid, perfect for a stroll and an easy starting point for exploring the city center.

Learn more
