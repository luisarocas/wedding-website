FAQ

If you have any additional questions, please feel free to text us directly.

When should I RSVP?

RSVPs will open in August 2026. The deadline to respond will be February 2027. We encourage you to reply as soon as possible once RSVPs open to help us with planning.

What is the dress code?

We invite our guests to dress in formal attire for the celebration. Gentlemen are encouraged to wear a suit and tie. Suit should ideally be in darker tones, and tie can be colorful. Ladies may choose long dresses in flowing fabrics like chiffon, silk, and tulle. Color is highly encouraged! We kindly ask guests to refrain from wearing white. If you'd like a few examples, you can view attire inspiration here.

Will transportation be provided?

Yes. Transportation will be provided to the venue and back at the end of the night. Details and timing will be shared closer to the date.

When should we book our flights to Madrid?

We recommend booking 6-9 months in advance for the best prices and availability.

What if I cannot attend?

We completely understand that international travel isn't possible for everyone. We're planning a separate celebration in Chicago, date to be determined, for those who can't join us in Spain, but please know we're grateful for your love and support in any form.

When should I arrive?

We recommend arriving at least one day before the wedding to allow time to settle in and adjust to travel. If you're coming from abroad, arriving two days before is ideal.

Is the wedding indoors or outdoors?

The ceremony will be held outdoors, weather permitting, followed by an indoor reception. Please plan accordingly.

Special dietary needs?

Please indicate any dietary restrictions when you RSVP, and we will do our best to accommodate you.
