Make the most of Madrid

Where to Stay

If you're spending a few days in Madrid, we recommend staying in the city, where you'll have more to see and do. Moncloa and Argüelles are especially practical because the wedding shuttle's first pickup will be in Moncloa.

You're welcome to stay wherever works best for you, including another Madrid neighborhood, an Airbnb, or Torrelodones. If you'd rather stay closer to the venue, Hotel Pax Torrelodones will be the shuttle's second stop.

Wedding transportation

Wedding Shuttle Stops

The wedding shuttle will make two pickup stops before continuing to the venue: first in the Moncloa area and then at Hotel Pax Torrelodones. The venue is outside Madrid, so allow around 30–45 minutes from the city, depending on traffic. Exact locations and timing will be confirmed closer to the wedding. You do not need to stay at one of these hotels to use the shuttle.

Getting here

Flights and Arrival

Airport

Adolfo Suárez Madrid-Barajas Airport (MAD) is Madrid's main international airport.

Arrival

We recommend arriving at least one day before the wedding. Two days before is ideal if you're traveling from abroad.

Important Information

ETIAS is not currently in operation. Because entry requirements may change before May 2027, please check the official website closer to your travel date.

Official ETIAS site

Transportation

Uber and taxis are widely available. Madrid's metro, buses, and trains are safe, affordable, and convenient.
