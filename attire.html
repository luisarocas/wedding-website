Dress code

Formal Attire

We invite our guests to dress in formal attire for the celebration. Gentlemen are encouraged to wear a suit and tie. Suit should ideally be in darker tones, and tie can be colorful. Ladies may choose long dresses in flowing fabrics like chiffon, silk, and tulle. Color is highly encouraged! We kindly ask guests to refrain from wearing white.

These images are meant to show the overall tone: polished, celebratory, elegant, and festive.
